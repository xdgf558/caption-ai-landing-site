## Promotional Text Draft

Turn breakfast, cafe, walking, pet, travel, and daily photos into share-ready captions with an on-device AI first experience.

## Short Description Draft

SnapCopy helps you create captions from everyday photos. Pick a photo, let the app understand the scene, generate caption options, copy or share, and rate results to shape your style.

## Keywords Draft

caption, captions, photo caption, AI caption, Instagram caption, social post, creator, photo writing, cafe, travel, pet, lifestyle

## Review Notes Draft

SnapCopy lets users select a photo and generate social-ready captions using Apple on-device AI where available. Users can copy or share generated text manually. The app does not automatically post to social platforms. If testing requires specific steps, open the app, choose a sample photo, tap generate, then copy/share/rate a caption.
