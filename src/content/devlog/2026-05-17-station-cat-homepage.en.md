This update turns the site from a single app landing page into a personal creative home for Station Cat.

The homepage now explains the station's personality: cats, meals, AI tools, internet signals, and strange but interesting ideas. SnapCopy stays on its own product page, where downloads, feature details, the waitlist, privacy policy, and support links can stay focused.

The language system is now usable across the site. Traditional Chinese remains the default, with Simplified Chinese, English, and Japanese available from the language menu. Future development updates, product experiments, and launch notes can live here in the dev blog.
